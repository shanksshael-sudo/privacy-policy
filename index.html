This Privacy Policy applies to the Vidya Sathi Kids and Vidya Sathi Admin applications (collectively referred to as "Vidya Sathi", "the App", "we", "us", or "our"), operated by ShanksCorp (accessible at shankscorp.site).

We are dedicated to safeguarding the privacy and digital security of students, teachers, parents, and school personnel. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how it is protected, and your rights regarding your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

Vidya Sathi is an educational school communication and learning platform. Accounts are strictly provisioned and issued by authorized school administrators. We collect only information necessary for academic administration:

  • Student Information: Student Name, unique Student ID, grade level, and Date of Birth.
  • Staff Information: Teacher Name, unique Staff ID, assigned grade, subject specialization, and institutional email address.
  • Academic & Activity Records: Homework completion statuses, assignment submission timestamps, class attendance registers, and academic notices.
  • User-Generated Educational Media: Optional photos or PDF documents uploaded by students or teachers strictly as homework attachments or worksheets.
  • AI Tutor Interactions: Conceptual questions asked to the kid-safe Chintu AI tutor for contextual learning assistance. Questions are processed ephemerally and are not used for behavioral profiling or advertising.

2. How We Use Information

All information collected is used exclusively for legitimate educational purposes, including:

  • Authenticating students and teachers into their designated school portals.
  • Facilitating the assignment, tracking, and completion of classroom homework.
  • Broadcasting school circulars, timetables, and critical institutional announcements.
  • Providing individualized, curriculum-aligned academic tutoring through Chintu AI.
  • Preventing unauthorized access and maintaining rigorous role-based access control.

3. Zero Data Sharing or Commercialization

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade any personal information or student data to third parties.

Our applications contain zero commercial third-party advertisements and zero third-party behavioral analytics tracking SDKs. Data is shared strictly with infrastructure providers that power the school service (such as encrypted database hosting and cloud storage) under strict data protection terms.

4. Children's Privacy & COPPA Compliance

Because Vidya Sathi Kids is utilized by primary and secondary school students, we adhere strictly to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Designed for Families policies:

  • We do not collect personal data from children without authorization from the educational institution and their parents/guardians.
  • Student accounts cannot be publicly created on the open internet; they are provisioned directly by the school.
  • We do not track or profile student behavior for commercial or promotional purposes.

5. Security & Data Storage

All data transmitted between the Vidya Sathi mobile apps and our backend infrastructure is encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Stored credentials use salted cryptographic one-way hashing (bcrypt). All file attachments are secured in access-controlled cloud object storage with time-limited signed access.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion Rights

Educational records are retained for the duration of the student's active enrollment at the participating school.
